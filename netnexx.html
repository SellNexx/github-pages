Frequently Asked Questions

What is NetNexx and how does it work?

NetNexx is a unified multi-vendor network management and monitoring platform that connects to your switches, routers, firewalls, and access points using standard protocols (SNMP, SSH, APIs) to give your IT operations one centralized console for discovery, telemetry, and alerting.

Can NetNexx be deployed on-premise?

Yes, NetNexx offers flexible deployment models: 100% SaaS Cloud, completely self-hosted On-premise within your secure data centers, or a Hybrid model with distributed local collectors reporting to a central dashboard.

Does NetNexx support multiple network vendors?

Yes, NetNexx is vendor-agnostic at its core. It normalizes data streams across heterogeneous multi-vendor hardware so your team never has to switch between disjointed vendor consoles.

How does NetNexx handle real-time alerting?

NetNexx constantly analyzes incoming telemetry streams against customizable thresholds and anomaly models, triggering instant notifications via Enterprise Chat, Alerting Webhooks, SMS, or Email before issues impact business end-users.
